## Question

The load-bearing open question from the destination interview: when a
department measures a goal gap, what FORCES the walk from gap → cause → new
sub-goal? Candidate shapes: (a) declared measurable chain per department,
(b) LLM-derived chain per walk, (c) a receipt gate: a gap CANNOT be escalated
without the stage that produced it attached. Agent lean: (c), matching
deny-by-default. Also in scope: the goal record schema (owner level, parent
ref, success criteria, guardrail-check receipt, audit-against-parent metric),
goal mutation mechanics (re-derive / transition plan / retire with history,
proof condition 5), and how the Goodhart audit runs without a human. Test
case: would this machinery, pointed at "pipeline 1 of 6" on 2026-08-03, have
emitted "harvest replies" as a sub-goal unprompted?
